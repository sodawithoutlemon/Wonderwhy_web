Legal bases

  • Providing the service you ask for (account, sync, answering questions, votes): performing our contract with you (KVKK Art. 5(2)(c)).
  • Security, misuse prevention, usage limits and logs: our legitimate interests (KVKK Art. 5(2)(f)).
  • Legal obligations, where they apply (KVKK Art. 5(2)(ç)).
  • Microphone and notifications: your permission, which you can withdraw in your phone’s settings. Before your first spoken question, the app tells you that your voice goes to Google’s AI and asks you to agree; nothing is recorded or sent until you do.

How we collect it

Electronically and automatically: what you enter when you create an account or sign in, what you create while using the app (lists, votes, questions) and the logs of requests to our servers. By email, if you write to us.

Who receives it

We don’t sell or share your personal data. To provide the service, these providers process it on our behalf:

  • Google (Firebase, Google Cloud, the Gemini API and Google sign-in): hosting, sign-in, sync, questions and audio.
  • Apple: Sign in with Apple, DeviceCheck and notifications.
  • GitHub: hosts this website.
  • {emailProvider}: hosts our mailbox.
  • OpenAI: writes the news summaries; it receives no personal data.

Our servers are in Belgium. Some of these providers process data outside Türkiye, including in the United States; these transfers rely on the mechanisms in Article 9 of the KVKK. Details: Who processes your data.

How long we keep it

Your account and what it holds stay until you delete the account. Everything else is deleted within 7 days at most. Details: How long we keep data.

Your rights under Article 11 of the KVKK

  • to learn whether your personal data is processed;
  • to request information about it if it is;
  • to learn the purpose of processing and whether it is used for that purpose;
  • to know the third parties it is transferred to in Türkiye or abroad;
  • to have it corrected if it is incomplete or wrong;
  • to have it deleted or destroyed under the conditions in Article 7 of the KVKK;
  • to have those third parties told about a correction, deletion or destruction;
  • to object to a result against you that comes only from automated analysis;
  • to claim compensation if unlawful processing causes you harm.

How to apply

To use these rights, apply to us in writing or write to {emailPrivacy} from the email address registered to your account. We may ask you to confirm that the request comes from you.
