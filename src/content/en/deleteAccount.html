This page explains how to delete your {brand} account and the data linked to it. {brand} is published by {publisher}.

In the app

  1. Open {brand} and go to the Profile tab.
  2. At the bottom, tap Delete account.
  3. Confirm with Delete account permanently.

Your account is deleted at once. If you signed in with Apple, you may be asked to confirm with your Apple ID, so that the app can also revoke its access to it.

If you subscribe: deleting your account doesn’t cancel your subscription. To stop being charged, cancel it separately in the App Store or Google Play subscription settings.

Without the app

If you no longer have the app or can’t sign in, write to {emailPrivacy} from your account’s email address, with the subject “Delete my account”. We delete the account and confirm by email within 30 days, usually much sooner. If you used Sign in with Apple and chose to hide your email, tell us the relay address Apple gave us, or write from your Apple ID’s address.

What is deleted

  • Your account: email address, name and sign-in details (with Google, also the web address of your profile photo).
  • Everything stored under it: preferences, saved stories, history, listening queue, the topics and sources you follow or mute, your word list and your listening progress.
  • All your votes and poll answers.
  • Your daily usage counters, the usage measurement on our servers, your email verification records and your and audio job records.
  • Your subscription status, your free listening count and your RevenueCat record.

What is kept, and for how long

  • Nothing linked to your account stays on our servers.
  • Usage and crash records in Google Analytics and Crashlytics are deleted on their own schedule: 2 months and 90 days.
  • Apple and Google keep purchase records under their own policies and legal obligations.
  • Ad data collected by Google AdMob isn’t linked to your account; Google deletes it on its own schedule.
  • Problem reports you sent aren’t linked to any account. They are deleted after 7 days.
  • Server logs (IP address, app and device details, no account ID) are deleted after 7 days.
  • On your phone, the app keeps its settings (topics, languages, text size) so it keeps working. It deletes audio downloaded for offline listening. Deleting the app removes them.

If you only looked around without an account, the guest ID the app may have created is deleted automatically after 7 days without use. The Privacy Policy lists how long we keep each kind of data.
