Your account

To open stories and listen, you need an account; without one you can go through the introduction and see the day’s headlines. When you create an account, we process your email address, your password and, if you enter it, your name. Google’s Firebase Authentication stores them; we never see your password. You can also use Sign in with Apple (on iPhone and iPad) or Sign in with Google. Apple or Google then shares your name, your email address (Apple may give a private relay address) and a sign-in token with us. With Google, Firebase Authentication also stores the web address of your Google profile photo; the app doesn’t use or show it.

Before you sign in, the app may create a random guest ID (a Firebase anonymous account) for features that need one. It isn’t linked to your name or email, but it is still personal data, because what is stored under it can be linked back to your phone. If you then create an account, your guest ID becomes your account ID. If you sign in to an account you already have, the app stops using the guest ID. A guest ID that isn’t used for 7 days is deleted automatically, together with its votes and counters.

What syncs to your account

When you are signed in with an account (not as a guest), the app keeps the following in Google Cloud Firestore so it follows you to your other devices. Only you can read it.

  • Preferences: your topics, languages, story depth, text size, listening settings and notification choices.
  • Saved stories (up to 50), stories you recently read or listened to (up to 30) and your listening queue (up to 20). These are copies of the stories inside the app.
  • Topics and sources you follow or mute.
  • Your own copy of your votes and poll answers, and the stories you reported.
  • Your word list and My questions (up to 1,500 entries): each word or concept with the sentence and headline where you found it. When you ask by voice what something means (for example “what is …?”), the concept and the answer are added here.
  • Today’s listening progress: which stories you heard, how long you listened and how many questions you asked. Only the number of questions is kept, not the questions.

Votes and reports

When you react to a story or answer a poll, your vote is stored with your account or guest ID, so that it counts once and you can change it. Other people see only the totals. Votes are deleted with the story, 7 days after the story’s day.

When you report a problem with a story or an answer, we store the story, the reason you picked and the note (up to 500 characters). If you report a voice answer, the app fills the note with your question and the answer so we can see what went wrong; you can change or delete it before you send. The report isn’t linked to your ID, and it is deleted after 7 days. Please don’t put personal information in the note: because it isn’t linked to you, we can’t find it earlier to delete it.

Microphone and questions

Push-to-talk questions

While a briefing plays, you can hold the microphone button and ask a question. The app records only while you hold the button, for up to 15 seconds. It sends the recording, together with the sentence that was playing, to our server. Our server sends them to the Google Gemini API, which works out your question and writes an answer. Google’s text-to-speech then reads the answer in the briefing’s voice.

Before your first voice question, the app tells you that your voice goes to Google’s AI and asks you to agree. Nothing is recorded or sent until you do.

We don’t store the recording, and we don’t write what you said or the answer to our logs. There are two exceptions, both in your hands: if you report an answer, the report keeps your question and the answer for 7 days (see Votes and reports); and when you ask what something means, the concept and the answer are saved to My questions in your Library and sync with your account until you delete it (see What syncs to your account). Our logs keep only numbers, such as the length of the answer and how long it took. Your voice is not used to identify you.

We use the Gemini API as a paid service. Under Google’s terms for paid services, Google doesn’t use this data to improve its products; it keeps prompts and answers for a limited time only to detect misuse and to meet legal obligations.

Ready-made questions

Some questions are buttons, such as asking for more detail on a story. Their answers are about the story, not about you, so we save them with the story and reuse them for everyone.

Your phone asks for your permission before the app can use the microphone. You can turn it off at any time in your phone’s settings.

How the news is made

Our server collects stories from news outlets’ public feeds, and reads the opening paragraphs of their pages to compare how outlets report the same story. It sends them to OpenAI to write the summaries and to Google to read them aloud. These requests contain only news text, none of your data. Stories and their audio files are deleted from our servers 7 days after the day they were published.

Your personal briefing is put together on your phone from the shared edition, so your topics aren’t sent to any AI service.

News images load directly from the publishers’ servers, and source links open the publisher’s page in a browser inside the app. Those publishers can see your IP address and apply their own privacy policies.

On your phone

  • Your lists, settings and sync state, in the app’s own storage.
  • A cache of story audio, in a temporary folder that your phone may clear.
  • Your sign-in session: on iPhone and iPad in the Keychain, where it can remain after you delete the app; on Android in the app’s private storage.
  • An offline copy of your synced data, kept by Firestore.

When you sign out or delete your account, the app removes your lists, votes, words and progress from the phone. It keeps your app settings (topics, languages, text size) so the app keeps working. Deleting the app removes them.

To protect our servers from misuse, the app uses Firebase App Check, with Apple’s DeviceCheck on iPhone and iPad and Google Play Integrity on Android, to confirm that requests come from the genuine app. We don’t store device identifiers.

Notifications

Only if you allow notifications, your phone subscribes to topics (such as your morning briefing time or major developments in your edition) through Firebase Cloud Messaging, and on iPhone and iPad also Apple’s push service. We send to topics, not to you personally, and we don’t store a device token. You can turn notifications off in the app’s Profile or in your phone’s settings.

Usage limits and server logs

To keep the service fair and affordable, we count how many questions you ask and how many story audios you have generated each day. Each counter holds your account or guest ID, the date and a number. While a story’s audio is being made, the job is recorded with your ID. Counters and job records are deleted after 7 days, and at once when you delete your account.

Our servers run on Google Cloud in Belgium (europe-west1). Google Cloud keeps request logs with IP addresses and app and device details for 7 days. Our own log lines contain numbers, not account IDs.

No ads, analytics or selling

The app has no advertising, no analytics and no crash-reporting tools, and it doesn’t track you across other companies’ apps or websites. We don’t sell or share personal data, as California law defines those terms. We don’t make decisions about you by automated means that have legal or similarly significant effects.

How long we keep data

Your account and what you keep in it stay until you delete the account. Everything else is deleted within 7 days: a job runs every night and removes what is older.

  • Account and synced data: until you delete your account.
  • Votes: deleted with the story, 7 days after the story’s day, or earlier if you delete your account.
  • Guest IDs: deleted after 7 days without use, together with their votes and counters. This includes a guest ID left behind when you signed in to an existing account.
  • Reports: 7 days.
  • Daily usage counters and audio job records: 7 days; deleted at once when you delete your account.
  • Voice recordings: not stored. A question and its answer are kept only as described above: in a report (7 days) or in My questions (until you delete your account).
  • Stories, story audio and ready-made answers: 7 days after the story’s day.
  • Server logs: 7 days. They contain no account IDs.
  • Emails you send us: as long as we need to deal with your message. If you ask us to tell you when the app is out, we keep your address until we have done so, then delete it.

Deleting your data

In the app, go to Profile › Delete account and confirm with Delete account permanently. This deletes at once your account, everything stored under it (lists, settings, words and progress), all your votes, and your usage counters and audio job records. If you used Sign in with Apple, the app also revokes its access to your Apple ID. On your phone, the app removes your lists and keeps its settings, as described above.

Afterwards nothing linked to your account remains on our servers. Anonymous reports and server logs, which contain no account ID, are deleted within 7 days.

If you can’t use the app, write to {emailPrivacy} from the email address of your account. The steps are also on the Delete your account page. Guest IDs are deleted automatically after 7 days without use; if you want yours deleted sooner, write to us.

Your rights

You can apply to us in writing or by email; we answer within 30 days, free of charge. If you aren’t satisfied, you can complain to the Personal Data Protection Authority (KVKK).
  • EEA and UK (GDPR): to access, correct or delete your data; to restrict or object to its use; to receive it in a portable format; to withdraw consent at any time; and to complain to your data protection authority.
  • California (CCPA/CPRA): to know what we collect and how we use it, to delete and correct it, and not to be treated differently for using these rights. We don’t sell or share personal information. We use sensitive information, such as your account login, only to provide the service.

To use any of these rights, write to {emailPrivacy}. We may ask you to confirm the request comes from you.

Legal bases

  • Providing the service you ask for (account, sync, answering questions, votes): performing our contract with you (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b); KVKK Art. 5(2)(c)).

  • Microphone and notifications: your permission, which you can withdraw in your phone’s settings (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a)).
  • Legal obligations, where they apply (GDPR Art. 6(1)(c); KVKK Art. 5(2)(ç)).
