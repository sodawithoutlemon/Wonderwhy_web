Example

Science · 1 source

Scientists discover chemosynthetic bacteria living on coral reef fish gills

Scientists have discovered chemosynthetic bacteria living on the gills of coral reef fish. While these bacteria were previously known to live in marine environments and alongside invertebrates like worms and mollusks, this is the first time they have been found on fish. Researchers believe this specialized microbiome could play a crucial role in fish metabolism, immunity, and health.

Sources: Phys.org

Example

Entertainment · 2 sources

Jon Watts to direct next Star Wars movie

A new Star Wars movie is in development with director Jon Watts attached. Watts previously directed the hit Spider-Man trilogy starring Tom Holland. Simon Kinberg is writing the project, which is reportedly part of a new trilogy that will serve as the next episodes of the Skywalker Saga.

Sources: Variety, The Hollywood Reporter

Questions

Frequently asked

When does it come out?

Soon. iPhone first, then Android. The store buttons on this page will work as soon as it’s out.

Which languages and editions are there?

The app is in Turkish and English. There are three editions: Türkiye (Turkish), United States (English) and World (English).

Who writes and reads the news?

AI writes the briefings from public news sources, and an AI voice reads them. Every story shows its sources. AI can make mistakes, so check the original when it matters.

Is my question recorded?

Your spoken question is sent to Google’s Gemini service so it can be understood and answered. We don’t store the recording, the transcript or the answer. The Privacy Policy has the details.

Where does the news come from?

From news outlets’ public feeds (RSS). The app groups stories that several outlets cover and shows where they differ.

Does it cost anything?

No. The app is free right now.
